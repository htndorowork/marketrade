How Payments Work on Marketrade

Last updated: 2026

In short: Marketrade only ever charges sellers a fixed subscription fee for access to selling tools. We do not process, hold, collect, or facilitate payment for any item sold between a buyer and a seller — that exchange happens directly between the two of them, entirely outside our systems.

What Marketrade charges for

Marketrade is a subscription platform. Sellers pay us directly for time-limited access to list items — plans currently range from R19 (48-hour Flash Sale) to R699 (6-month Semester Pass). This is the only thing our payment integration (Paystack) is used for. The fee is for the use of our software and listing visibility, not for any specific item a seller later sells.

What Marketrade does not charge for, hold, or touch

  • We never process a card, EFT, or any other payment for the actual item a buyer is purchasing from a seller.
  • We never receive, hold, or move funds on behalf of a buyer or seller for a transaction between them.
  • We are not a party to that transaction and have no Paystack, banking, or escrow relationship covering it.
  • Buyers and sellers arrange and settle payment for goods directly with each other — typically via cash, EFT, or another method they agree on — coordinated through our in-app messaging or WhatsApp.

Why this distinction matters

Marketrade is a classifieds-style listing platform with paid seller subscriptions — comparable to a SaaS subscription business. We are not a payment facilitator, payment aggregator, or marketplace payment processor: we never route, split, or hold funds intended for a third party. Any card or EFT payment made through Marketrade goes to Marketrade, for Marketrade's own subscription service, in full.
